# Risk Management Plan

## Risk 1 - Missed Deadlines

Team members may miss deadlines, delaying project progress.

Mitigation: Weekly check-ins and clearly assigned responsibilities.

## Risk 2 - Unclear Requirements

Unclear project requirements may cause rework.

Mitigation: Review project description and confirm requirements with the team.

## Risk 3 - Uneven Work Distribution

Some team members may have heavier workloads.

Mitigation: Assign tasks based on each member’s skills and availability.

## Risk 4 - Technical Issues

Collaboration tools or file sharing may fail.

Mitigation: Use multiple communication channels and shared document backups.

## Risk 5 - Scheduling Conflicts

Team members may have different schedules.

Mitigation: Flexible meeting times and asynchronous updates.
